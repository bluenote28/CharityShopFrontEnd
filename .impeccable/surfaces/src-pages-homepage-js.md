# Home

mode: Operate
audience: Shoppers finding purchases that benefit charity
job: Start from search or a department, then go to eBay. Further down, open a non-eBay shop from the directory.
constraints: Checkout stays on eBay. Benefits stays obvious on every listing. Do not invent charities, prices, or donate/sell flows. Other-shop names and descriptions come only from the directory. Logo and Charity Shop name stay.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Home is a marketplace search surface, not a donation appeal and not an icon-tile poster. It refuses the old Bootstrap category-icon landing.

OWN-WORLD: White page, slate type, one marketplace-blue primary. Compact top bar, a dominant Search all items field, department chips, then a featured-listing row whose charity badge is a small heart-label on the card. eBay/Amazon craft: dense, familiar, no experimental identity.

STORY: The visitor understands this is a shop, believes they can find an item that helps a charity, and searches or taps a department. After the category reels, one shelf shows the directory's other shops, each opening that shop, with the full directory one step away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logo and account links in a slim white header. Search all items is the largest control, with a blue Search button. A chip row of departments plus Charities. Featured Listings as four product cards with photo, charity badge, title, category, price. View all listings is secondary.

FORM: Category-standard marketplace, seed a306e2d2, kind canon, composition home-search. Approved comp `.impeccable/mocks/home-comp-2.webp`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
